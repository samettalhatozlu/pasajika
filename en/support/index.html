Frequently asked questions

How do I start?

On first launch tap Kepengi Kaldır (Raise the shutter). Wipe the dusty window with your finger; when it sparkles, read the master's note and tap Rafı Düzenle (Arrange the shelf). No sign-up or login is needed.

What do the shelf rules mean?

The shelf is complete when every note of the master is satisfied at the same time. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

  • Yan yana (side by side): on the same shelf, in neighbouring cells.
  • Hemen üstünde (directly above): in the same column, one cell higher.
  • Daha üst bir raf (a higher shelf): any column, on a higher shelf.
  • Daha solda (further left): any shelf, in a column further left.
I'm stuck. What can I do?

A broken note turns red. The lightbulb works in two steps: the first tap highlights the next item and the relevant notes (no star lost); the second tap places it or takes a misplaced item back to the counter. The curved arrow next to the notes undoes your last move and the circular arrow empties the shelf. With the pencil you can turn on notes mode and mark cells where an item cannot go.

How are mistakes, hints and stars counted?

Placing an item where it breaks one of the notes counts as a mistake. Hints that place an item count as hints (the first, highlight-only tap does not). You earn 1–3 stars: no hints and at most 1 mistake for 3 stars; at most 1 hint and 3 mistakes for 2 stars; any other solve gives 1 star.

What is an order book?

When you restore a shop, its order book opens: 10 customer orders from Easy to Master, 70 levels across the seven shops. Orders unlock one after another and your best stars and time are kept. Tap the shop in the list or its door in the courtyard to open the book.

What are Free Play and the daily archive?

Serbest Oyun (Free Play) gives you unlimited new puzzles in Easy, Medium or Master. Geçmiş Siparişler (Past Orders) lets you solve Daily Orders you missed, from up to the last 30 days; archive solves don't change your streak. Both open from cards in the courtyard.

What is Check Mode?

An optional, harder way to play, turned on in Settings. While it's on, notes don't turn red as you place items and no mistakes are counted; arrange the shelf and tap Kontrol Et (Check) to see which notes fail. Each failed check counts as one mistake.

When does the Daily Order change?

Every day at midnight in your device's local time. Everyone solves the same puzzle on the same day. Difficulty follows the week: Monday and Tuesday are easy, Wednesday to Friday medium, the weekend master level.

How does the streak work?

Your streak grows by one for each day you solve the Daily Order. Skip a day and it starts again; your best streak is kept separately. Past orders solved from the archive don't change the streak.

Where is my progress stored? Does it move to a new device?

Your progress is stored only on your device; there is no account or server. When you restore your device backup (iCloud or computer) to a new device, your progress comes with it. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I turn off sound and haptics?

Open Settings with the gear icon in the courtyard and use the ASMR sesleri (sounds) and Titreşim (haptics) switches. In silent mode the game plays no sound and doesn't interrupt your music.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings › İlerlemeyi sıfırla (Reset progress). Shops, letters, orders, stars, Free Play results, the daily streak, achievements and statistics are erased; your sound, haptics and Check Mode preferences are kept. This can't be undone.

Which devices are supported?

Every iPhone with iOS 17 or later and every iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. The game works in portrait and landscape, in windowed mode on iPad and on both displays of iPhone Duo, without an internet connection.

Is there accessibility support?

Yes. Items, shelf cells and rules are read by VoiceOver, and you can place an item by selecting it and then a cell instead of dragging. The window cleaning has a VoiceOver action to clean the whole window, and the game adapts to larger text sizes.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Pasajika is free, with no ads, in-app purchases or tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
